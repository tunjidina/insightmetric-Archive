# Overview

## What InsightMetric is

InsightMetric is a lightweight daily trend-intelligence dashboard. It tracks emerging
signals across **AI, cybersecurity, data science and technology**, organises them into clear
topics, and shows which ones look likely to still be around tomorrow.

It opens on today. A ranked list of what is moving, each entry carrying a sense of how it is
being discussed, how widely it is being covered, and how likely it is to persist. A weekly
view steps back and shows which topics held their place and which disappeared.

## The problem it addresses

Anyone who follows technology closely has the same two difficulties.

**There is too much of it.** A dozen outlets publishing continuously produces far more than
anyone can read, and most of it is the same handful of stories told repeatedly. What is
scarce is not information but a sense of proportion.

**Almost none of it is measured.** Coverage tells you what happened; it rarely tells you
whether the thing mattered. A story can dominate a morning and be forgotten by the
afternoon, and nothing in the reading experience distinguishes the two at the time.

InsightMetric addresses both by narrowing the question. Not *what is important* — a question
no dashboard should claim to answer — but *what is still here tomorrow*, which is specific,
falsifiable, and can be checked the next day.

## Who it is for

- **Practitioners** in AI, security and data who want a two-minute morning read rather than
  a feed
- **Analysts and writers** who need to see what a week actually contained before writing
  about it
- **Anyone curious** about whether the noise around a technology topic is signal

You do not need a technical background to use it. Every number on screen is explained in
plain language in [USAGE.md](USAGE.md).

## Vision

A daily reading of the technology landscape that is short enough to actually read, honest
about what it does not know, and gets better the longer it runs.

Ambitions worth stating out loud:

- **A record, not just a snapshot.** The value compounds. A single day is a list; a year is
  a history you can ask questions of.
- **Legible by default.** Anyone should be able to understand what a number on screen means
  and how much weight to put on it. An estimate nobody can interpret is not useful.
- **Honest about uncertainty.** Where the product is unsure, it says so, rather than
  rounding a guess up into a claim.

## Philosophy

> **Simple tools → powerful insights.**

The product grows over time, and it improves as its history grows. Small, understandable
components, assembled so that each can be improved on its own without rebuilding the rest.

Three commitments follow from that:

**Start with the simplest thing that works.** A modest, well-understood approach that can be
explained in a sentence beats an elaborate one that cannot. Complexity has to earn its place
by making the product measurably better, not by sounding impressive.

**Say what you do not know.** Estimates come with an indication of confidence. Where the
data is thin, the documentation says so. The section on sample data in the README exists
because it would be easy and dishonest to leave out.

**Build so it can be replaced.** Every part is designed to be swapped for a better version
later. What is here today is a foundation, not a ceiling.

The simplest tools today enable powerful insights tomorrow.

## Where it is going

InsightMetric is early. The near-term direction:

- **More history.** The single most valuable thing the product can do right now is keep
  running. Estimates improve with the record behind them.
- **Better explanations.** Showing not just what is estimated but why, in language a
  non-specialist can follow.
- **More ways in.** Search across history, richer comparison, and views that answer a
  question rather than presenting a page.
